About this app

Every medicine sold in India lists its salt (generic composition) on the pack, but safety information is usually written for the salt, not the brand. This app lets you type the salt and see the official safety information in plain, scannable cards.

How it works

  1. You type the salt. Indian and UK names are mapped to the US names openFDA uses, for example Paracetamol becomes Acetaminophen.
  2. We fetch the data. The drug label gives official warnings, uses, interactions and pregnancy notes. Adverse event reports show how often each reaction was reported.

Data source

  • openFDA – US FDA drug labels and adverse event reports (FAERS). It covers drugs sold in the US, so some India-only salts will not be found.
Medical disclaimer

This app is for general information only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Adverse event counts show what was reported, not how likely a reaction is, and reports do not prove a drug caused the reaction. US labels may differ from Indian ones. Always ask a doctor or pharmacist before starting, changing or stopping any medicine. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
